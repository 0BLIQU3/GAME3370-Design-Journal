Fortnite

Choose one mechanic from Fortnite and explain how it communicates the game’s intended audience or theme. How might you redesign that mechanic, goal, or interface for a different audience? Explain what experience you want the redesigned version to create

Design

One key aspect of Fortnite is the storm in its battle royale format: it serves as a way to encourage more player interaction as numbers dwindle by concentrating them in an increasingly smaller area. Now, I do think this is perfectly effective for Fortnite's current purposes of engaging more combat out of players consistently, but of course, the challenge here is to potentially engage a different audience, so I think it would be valid to focus on the sort of "race against time" aspect of the storm, when you're pushed to its edge and may be split between shooting down an enemy or simply getting further away. Now, it's my understanding that the way that spawning works is that players may spawn anywhere along a straight line across the map governed by the Battle Bus, so some players may end up needing to run from the storm less than others.

To elicit this sort of "race against time" aspect out of all players, I think it could be good to change spawn locations to occur anywhere around the edge of the storm, far away from the eye's center. This should encourage players to consider the risk/reward between beelining for the center or getting loot/fighting other players: a simple example would be whether or not you choose to use your building materials for a wall to protect from another player in a fight or a staircase to traverse terrain quicker. This approach to gameplay can be supported by having increasingly better loot spawn near the center (perhaps a special grand loot cache at the very center?), special loot drops dotted along the way that generally take some time investment (so people racing towards the center may optionally drop their lead for this loot), rewards that specifically improve movement that may require backtracking into the storm (like the Grapple Gun), or new items capable of obstructing progress for others. Ideally, this can help appeal to players who value good movement and routing over strict combat.
