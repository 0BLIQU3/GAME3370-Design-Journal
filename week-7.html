Path Out

Compare how Fined Out and Path Out use mechanics, feedback, choice, or space to communicate a social issue. Which design is more effective for its intended purpose, and why? Identify one risk of turning a complex social issue into a game and one design decision that could address that risk.

Design

When it comes to communicate social issues, Fined Out will often have you experience a dilemma and then offer some form of informational note afterwards to provide context for how the situation may be reflected in real life. Path Out, on the other hand, may communicate this via YT-style commentary from the creator after a scenario. Personally, I believe that Fined Out's method is far more effective, in part due to Path Out's commentary lacking educational substance and breaking immersion with the game. Youtube commentary has been a popular way to analyze the events of a game throughout the year, yes, but I believe that this approach is flawed for Path Out because the practice of YT commentary was never something to serve the original game experience in the first place. YT commentary is something that happens entirely separately from any initial playthrough of a game, and having a person's real face appear in the corner of your game's screen is something that can take you out of the experience entirely. Fined Out, on the other hand, at least maintained a degree of immersion by matching its critical context with the aesthetics of the game's UI/artstyle, allowing for a player to remain invested in the game as a whole.

When it comes to turning a complex social issue into a game, I believe that care must be taken to not undermine the nature of it being a game in the first place, as at that point, you might as well have just chosen some avenue other than a game to convey this. The game is not merely a means to an end to convey information: it is a unique form of art that should be able to inherently reflect the issue at hand. To this, I was a bit disappointed at Path Out's usage of RPGmaker: it does not really take advantage of RPGmaker's systems and, for all of its choices in dialogue to try to convey how precarious Abdullah's situation was, it is not simple to view the outcome of other choices to actually see this gravity. The usage of default assets seemed all the more apparent, and I would consider Path Out to effectively have "boilerplate" mechanics with RPGmaker, whereas Fined Out is much more efficient with its gameplay. I think that, to address the risk of undermining the game, it would've been good for Path Out to at least take more care into integrating the YT-style commentary into the game's aesthetics. However, I don't think styling it like Youtube was very necessary at all, though I suppose if only the words were conveyed minus the face, it would've drawn too much attention to how...lackluster I found some of the actual comments (ex: comments like "oh no you killed me, that wasn't very cool" when you die in the game, distinctly interrupting some of the more serious/profound-sounding text displayed beforehand).
